# Jivvy brand guide

## The logo
The wordmark is lowercase **jivvy** in heavy, square-cut letters. Its one signature detail is **the slice**: the first v cuts into the i along a matching diagonal, leaving an even gap. Everything else stays deliberately plain so the slice is the moment people remember.

Rules the letters follow:
- One stroke weight across every letter
- Square dots and flat, square-cut ends
- The only curves are the j's hook and the small rounded corner on the i
- The slice angle (about 18° from vertical) is the same angle as the v arms

## Colors
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Night navy | `#10224F` | Primary background, type on light backgrounds |
| Paper | `#F4F1EA` | Letters on navy, light backgrounds |
| Jivvy blue | `#2E6BFF` | Brand color for surfaces, links, highlights |
| On air orange | `#FF7A1A` | The i, the slash, and small accents only |

Keep orange small. It should only ever be a spark: the i, the slash, a dot, a badge. Never a large area.

## Sub-brands
Each division uses the same lockup: **jivvy / NAME**. The slash is orange and leans at the slice angle; the name is set in DM Mono Medium, all caps, widely spaced (converted to outlines in the files).

| Lockup | Covers |
|---|---|
| Jivvy / Live | Live events and streaming |
| Jivvy / Systems | IT infrastructure |
| Jivvy / Stage | Audio and stage design |
| Jivvy / Advisory | IT recommendations and consulting |

## App icon
The **j** on its own, with the dot in orange, on navy. Delivered as a full-bleed 1024 × 1024 square; iOS and Android round the corners themselves.

## Brand system ideas (next)
- **Live level meter:** in motion, the orange fills up the i like an audio level when something goes live.
- **The slash as divider:** breadcrumbs, separators and dividers lean at the slice angle.
- **Angled corner cuts:** clip one corner of photos, cards and buttons at the same angle.
